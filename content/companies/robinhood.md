Robinhood's frontend phone screen has a recurring question and a level-based grading rubric: candidates are expected to clear different stages of the same exercise depending on the level they're interviewing for.

## JavaScript coding questions

- Implement an **event emitter** progressively in 3 stages of increasing complexity. Senior / staff candidates are expected to clear all 3 stages.
  - [Practice question](https://www.greatfrontend.com/questions/javascript/event-emitter) (Free)

## Insider tips from the GreatFrontEnd community

These tips were shared by [GreatFrontEnd](https://www.greatfrontend.com/) users who have completed interviews with Robinhood.

**March 2026**:

> Robinhood's phone screen uses their common question — implement event emitter in 3 stages. They expect you to clear all 3 if you're senior/staff. I got to 2.5 (very close to completing the last level) and didn't pass. Don't underestimate it: I went in thinking it wouldn't be a big deal and wasn't seriously trying hard. Treat all 3 stages as required.
