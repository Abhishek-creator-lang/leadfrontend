> "How do you decide between paying down technical debt and building new features? Tell me about a specific time you had to make that call."

Every front-end lead faces this: an ageing build, flaky tests, a tangled state layer, and a roadmap full of features. Interviewers want to see that you treat debt as a business decision, can quantify its cost, and can negotiate a sustainable approach with product rather than either ignoring debt or demanding a rewrite.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Quantifying debt**: can you express its cost in delivery speed, incidents or user impact?
- **Prioritisation**: which debt is worth paying, and which can stay?
- **Negotiation**: how you got product and leadership to agree.
- **Execution model**: dedicated capacity, opportunistic refactoring, or a focused project.
- **Proving the payoff**: did you show results afterward?

## Common variants

- "How do you convince product to invest in refactoring?"
- "Tell me about a time you chose to take on tech debt deliberately."
- "How much of your team's time goes to maintenance, and how did you decide?"
- "Your team wants to rewrite the app. What do you do?"
