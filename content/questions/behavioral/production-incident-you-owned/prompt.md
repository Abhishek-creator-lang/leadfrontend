> "Tell me about a production incident you were responsible for handling. Walk me through it from the moment you found out."

Front-end incidents are often subtle: a blank page for one browser, a broken checkout button behind a CDN cache, a third-party script taking down the main thread. Interviewers want to see calm, structured response under pressure, clear communication and a post-incident process that fixes the system, not the person.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Incident handling**: detection, triage, mitigation before root cause, rollback strategy.
- **Communication**: status updates to stakeholders and support while the incident is live.
- **Technical depth**: how you found the real cause.
- **Ownership without blame**: owning the response even if someone else wrote the bug.
- **Systemic follow-up**: blameless postmortem and changes that prevent a whole class of incident.

## Common variants

- "Tell me about the worst outage you've been involved in."
- "Describe a time something you shipped broke production."
- "How do you run an incident as incident commander?"
- "What's your approach to postmortems?"
