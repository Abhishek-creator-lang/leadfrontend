> "Tell me about a time you needed several teams, who did not report to you, to agree on something and actually do it. How did you make it happen?"

Front-end leads often own things that cut across teams: shared libraries, performance, accessibility, analytics, platform upgrades. This question tests whether you can coordinate people with competing roadmaps and get to a decision that sticks.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Understanding incentives**: what each team cared about and why they might resist.
- **Structuring the decision**: clear options, criteria, a decision owner and a deadline.
- **Communication**: written proposals, the right meetings, follow-ups.
- **Making it easy to say yes**: tooling, migration help, sequencing that fits other roadmaps.
- **Follow-through**: tracking adoption, handling stragglers, closing the loop.

## Common variants

- "Describe a project that depended on other teams. How did you manage the dependencies?"
- "How do you get teams to adopt a shared standard?"
- "Tell me about a time teams disagreed on an approach and you brought them together."
- "How do you coordinate a breaking change across many consumers?"
