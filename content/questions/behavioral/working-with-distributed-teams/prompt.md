> "Our front-end team is spread across three time zones. Tell me about a time you led or worked in a distributed team. What did you change to make it work?"

Most lead roles now involve people you rarely see in person. The interviewer is looking for habits and systems that work asynchronously, not for "we had a lot of video calls".

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Async-first communication**: written decisions, clear handoffs, documentation people actually use.
- **Fairness across time zones**: who carries the cost of meetings and late-night pages.
- **Unblocking**: how work keeps moving when the person with the answer is asleep.
- **Inclusion and trust**: making sure remote or offshore engineers get real ownership, not leftovers.
- **Measuring the effect**: evidence that your changes improved flow.

## Common variants

- "How do you build trust with people you have never met in person?"
- "Tell me about a challenge working with an offshore or contractor team."
- "How do you run code review and decision-making across time zones?"
- "What does a good async culture look like to you?"
