> "Tell me about a time you pushed for accessibility on a product. What was the situation, who did you need to convince, and what changed?"

Accessibility is often the first thing cut under pressure. Leads are expected to make it part of how the team works: in design reviews, components, CI and definition of done. This question checks whether you can do that and bring others with you.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Knowledge**: WCAG levels, assistive technology testing, and common failure patterns.
- **Influence**: how you persuaded product, design and leadership, especially when there was no legal deadline.
- **Systemic thinking**: fixing the design system and process, not only individual bugs.
- **Empathy**: whether you involved disabled users or their real experiences.
- **Measurement**: audits, automated checks, issue counts or task-completion data.

## Common variants

- "How do you make sure your team builds accessible products?"
- "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a designer about accessibility."
- "How would you introduce accessibility to a team that has ignored it?"
- "What is an accessibility issue you are proud of fixing?"
