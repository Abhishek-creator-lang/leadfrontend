> "Tell me about someone you helped grow from a strong senior engineer into a tech lead. What did you actually do?"

At staff and lead level, part of your impact is measured by the leaders you create. The interviewer wants to see a deliberate method, not "I gave them good advice in one-to-ones".

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Diagnosis**: can you name the specific gap between a senior and a lead for this person?
- **Creating opportunities**: did you give them real scope, such as a project, a stakeholder relationship or an RFC?
- **Coaching style**: feedback loops, modelling, and stepping back at the right time.
- **Sponsorship**: did you use your influence to get them visibility and promotion?
- **Measuring growth**: evidence that it worked, beyond your opinion.

## Common variants

- "How do you develop future leaders on your team?"
- "What is the difference between a senior engineer and a lead, and how do you coach someone across it?"
- "Tell me about someone you sponsored for promotion."
- "Have you ever had someone not ready for a lead role who wanted it? What did you do?"
