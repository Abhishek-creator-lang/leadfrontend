> "Tell me about a time you raised the engineering quality bar on your team. What was wrong, what did you change, and how did you get people to adopt it?"

Every lead says they care about quality. This question checks whether you can turn that into a system: tests, CI gates, review norms, and observability that the team actually keeps using once you stop pushing.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Diagnosis**: did you find the real cause of poor quality, or just add more tests everywhere?
- **Pragmatism**: the right test types in the right places, and gates that do not slow everyone down.
- **Change management**: how you got buy-in from engineers who were under delivery pressure.
- **Measurement**: escaped defects, change failure rate, flaky test rate, lead time.
- **Sustainability**: whether the improvements survived after the push.

## Common variants

- "How do you improve code quality on a team without slowing it down?"
- "Tell me about introducing a testing strategy."
- "What have you done to reduce bugs reaching production?"
- "How do you define 'done' for your team?"
