> "Tell me about the hardest bug you have ever tracked down. Why was it hard, and how did you find it?"

A favourite opener because it is technical and behavioural at once. The interviewer learns how you think under uncertainty, how deep your platform knowledge goes, and whether you fix only the symptom or the system around it.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Method**: forming hypotheses, reproducing, narrowing down, using the right tools.
- **Technical depth**: browser internals, framework behaviour, networking, build tooling.
- **Persistence and prioritisation**: how you balanced the bug against other work, and when you asked for help.
- **Communication**: how you kept affected users or teams informed.
- **Prevention**: tests, monitoring or guardrails so the class of bug does not come back.

## Common variants

- "Describe a bug that took you a long time to understand."
- "Tell me about a time you debugged something you could not reproduce locally."
- "What's the most interesting production issue you have investigated?"
- "Walk me through how you debug a problem that only some users see."
