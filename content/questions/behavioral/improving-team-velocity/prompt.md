> "Tell me about a time your team was shipping too slowly. How did you figure out why, and what did you change?"

Every lead is asked to make their team faster. Interviewers want to see that you diagnose before prescribing, that you measure the right things (not story points), and that you improve the system rather than pushing people to work harder.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Diagnosis**: did you find the actual bottleneck with data, or apply a favourite fix?
- **Metrics literacy**: lead time, deployment frequency, PR cycle time, CI duration, change failure rate, and the danger of gaming them.
- **Systems thinking**: process, tooling, architecture and team structure all affect speed.
- **Influence**: getting the team and management to invest time in improvement.
- **Sustainability**: speed gained without burning people out or lowering quality.

## Common variants

- "How do you measure engineering productivity?"
- "Your team's velocity has been falling for two quarters. What do you do?"
- "Tell me about a developer-experience improvement you drove."
- "How have you reduced the time from commit to production?"
