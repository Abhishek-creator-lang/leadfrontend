> "Tell me about a time you had to cut scope on a project. How did you decide what to cut, and how did you get product and design to agree?"

Scope negotiation is a core lead skill. The interviewer wants to see that you treat scope as a tool, that you can propose cuts grounded in user value and risk, and that you bring partners along instead of announcing decisions to them.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Prioritisation framework**: how you separate what users need from what is nice to have.
- **Partnership**: whether product and design felt heard, and whether the relationship survived.
- **Creativity**: offering alternatives (phasing, simpler versions, feature flags) instead of plain "no".
- **Data use**: analytics, research or support data to back up the decision.
- **Follow-through**: did the cut items get revisited, or silently dropped?

## Common variants

- "How do you handle scope creep?"
- "Tell me about a time you had to deliver less than what was asked."
- "Describe a time you pushed back on a design because of time constraints."
- "How do you decide what makes the MVP?"
