> "Tell me about a time two people on your team were in conflict. What did you do, and how did it turn out?"

This is a people-leadership question. The interviewer wants to know whether you notice conflict early, whether you can find what is really going on beneath the surface, and whether you act fairly without taking sides or avoiding the problem.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Awareness**: did you notice early, or only once it blew up?
- **Listening**: hearing each person separately and finding the underlying cause.
- **Fairness**: not siding with the more senior or more vocal person.
- **Action**: changes to roles, processes or norms, not just a "talk it out" meeting.
- **Judgement on escalation**: when to involve a manager or HR.
- **Outcome**: the working relationship and the team's health afterwards.

## Common variants

- "How do you handle conflict within your team?"
- "Tell me about a time code reviews became tense or personal."
- "Describe a situation where two strong engineers could not work together."
- "What do you do when someone complains to you about a colleague?"
