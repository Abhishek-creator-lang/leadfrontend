> "Tell me about a performance improvement you led. How did you know it mattered to the business, what did you do, and how did you measure the result?"

Front-end leads are expected to connect performance work to outcomes the business cares about. The interviewer wants technical depth on the fix, but also how you made the case, prioritised it and stopped the gains from slowly disappearing.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Measurement**: field data (RUM, CrUX) vs lab data (Lighthouse), and which percentiles you used.
- **Diagnosis**: finding the real bottleneck instead of applying a generic checklist.
- **Business link**: conversion, engagement, SEO or cost, ideally proven with an experiment.
- **Influence**: how you got time on the roadmap for performance work.
- **Sustainability**: performance budgets, CI checks and ownership after the project.

## Common variants

- "Tell me about the biggest performance problem you have solved."
- "How have you improved Core Web Vitals on a product?"
- "How do you convince product managers to invest in performance?"
- "Describe a time you used data to justify technical work."
