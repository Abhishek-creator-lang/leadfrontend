> "So, why are you looking to leave your current role?"

This is a short question with a lot of risk. Interviewers are listening for bitterness, blame, or a pattern that will repeat at their company. A good answer is honest, brief and points forward.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Professionalism**: can you talk about a past employer without complaining?
- **Self-awareness**: do you understand what you need from a job?
- **Repeat risk**: is the reason something that would also make you leave this role?
- **Honesty**: does the story hold together with your CV and the rest of the interview?
- **Positive motivation**: are you moving towards something specific?

## Common variants

- "Why did you leave your last company?"
- "I see you were only at your last job for a year. What happened?"
- "What would make you stay where you are?"
- "You were affected by a layoff. Can you tell me about that?"
