> "Tell me about a time you or your team were close to burning out. How did you notice, and what did you do about it?"

Leads set the pace for a team. This question checks whether you see people's wellbeing as part of delivery rather than something that competes with it, and whether you can make structural changes, not just tell people to take a day off.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Noticing early**: the signals you watch for in people and in data.
- **Root cause**: whether you found the source of the overload (planning, on-call, unclear priorities).
- **Structural change**: capacity planning, rotation, scope, or pushing back on leadership.
- **Modelling behaviour**: whether you keep a sustainable pace yourself.
- **Delivery**: showing that sustainable pace and good output go together.

## Common variants

- "How do you keep your team from burning out during a crunch?"
- "Tell me about a time you had too much on your plate."
- "How do you protect your team from pressure from above?"
- "How do you manage your own energy as a lead?"
