> "Tell me about a time you had to deliver something on a deadline that felt unrealistic. How did you approach it, and how did it turn out?"

Interviewers ask this to see how you behave under pressure. For a lead, the interesting part is not working late; it is how you shaped the plan, managed risk, communicated, and kept the team healthy.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Planning under constraint**: breaking work down, finding the critical path, and spotting risk early.
- **Trade-off judgement**: what you cut, what you protected (security, accessibility, data integrity), and why.
- **Communication**: how and when you told stakeholders the truth about the plan.
- **Team leadership**: how you used people's strengths and avoided burning them out.
- **Outcome**: did you hit the date, and what did it cost?

## Common variants

- "Describe a time you were under significant time pressure."
- "Tell me about a launch with a fixed date you could not move."
- "How do you handle a deadline you think is wrong?"
- "Tell me about a time you had to ship faster than you were comfortable with."
