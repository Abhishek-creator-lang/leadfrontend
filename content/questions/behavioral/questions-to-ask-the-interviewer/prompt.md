> "We have about five minutes left. What questions do you have for me?"

Every interview ends this way. At senior and lead level, interviewers do notice what you ask: the questions reveal what you care about and how you would think in the role. It is also your best chance to find out whether you want the job.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Curiosity and preparation**: questions that build on what you learned earlier in the interview.
- **Lead-level thinking**: interest in how decisions are made, how teams work together and what success looks like.
- **Judgement**: asking things that this particular interviewer can answer.
- **Engagement**: "No, I think you covered everything" signals low interest.

## Common variants

- "Is there anything you would like to know about the team?"
- "What else can I tell you about the role?"
- In a hiring manager round: "What would help you decide if this is the right job for you?"
