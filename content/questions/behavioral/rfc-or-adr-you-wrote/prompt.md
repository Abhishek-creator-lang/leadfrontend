> "Tell me about a design document, RFC or architecture decision record you wrote. What problem did it address, what options did you consider, and how did you get to a decision?"

At staff and lead level, much of your impact comes through writing. Interviewers use this question to judge how you frame problems, compare options fairly, handle feedback and turn a document into an actual decision and rollout.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Problem framing**: a clear statement of the problem, the constraints and what is out of scope.
- **Option analysis**: real alternatives with honest trade-offs, including "do nothing".
- **Feedback process**: who reviewed it, how you handled objections, and how you changed your mind.
- **Decision-making**: who decided, how, and how the decision was recorded.
- **Outcome**: whether the decision held up, and whether you revisited it.

## Common variants

- "How do you make large technical decisions on your team?"
- "Tell me about a time you wrote a proposal that changed how your organisation built things."
- "What makes a good design document?"
- "Describe a technical decision you documented and later had to revisit."
