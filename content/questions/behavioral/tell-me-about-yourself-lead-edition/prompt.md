> "Let's start with you. Tell me a bit about yourself and what brings you to this role."

Almost every loop opens with this. It looks like small talk, but for a lead role it is your first chance to set the level the interviewer will judge you at. A weak opening makes them probe for scope for the rest of the hour. A strong one gives them the hooks they will ask about next.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Level signal**: do you describe your work in terms of team, product and business impact, or only tasks and tools?
- **Narrative**: is there a clear thread through your career that leads to this role?
- **Communication**: can you be concise, structured and confident in about two minutes?
- **Self-awareness**: do you know what you are good at and what you want to grow into?
- **Motivation and fit**: why this role, why now.

## Common variants

- "Walk me through your resume."
- "Give me the two-minute version of your career."
- "What are you working on right now, and what's your role in it?"
- "Introduce yourself as you would to a new team you're about to lead."
