> "What's your experience with hiring? Tell me about a time you changed how your team interviews or made a difficult hiring decision."

Leads shape their team through hiring. The interviewer wants to know whether you can design a fair, predictive process, train others to run it, and make hard calls without bias.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Process design**: interviews that reflect the real job, with clear rubrics.
- **Fairness**: structured interviews, consistent questions, reducing bias.
- **Calibration**: training interviewers and aligning on what "senior" means.
- **Decision-making**: handling split feedback, saying no to a strong-but-wrong candidate.
- **Candidate experience**: respecting time, giving a good impression even to rejected candidates.
- **Measuring outcomes**: did hires succeed?

## Common variants

- "How do you evaluate a senior front-end engineer in an interview?"
- "Tell me about a hire you made that didn't work out."
- "How would you design a front-end interview loop from scratch?"
- "Describe a time interviewers disagreed on a candidate. What did you do?"
