> "Have you ever set the technical direction for a group of teams? How did you come up with it, how did you get people on board, and how did you know it was working?"

This is a staff-level question. The interviewer wants evidence that you can look beyond the next sprint, connect technical choices to business strategy, and lead through writing and influence rather than authority.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Strategic thinking**: grounding the vision in business goals and current pain, not trends.
- **Research**: talking to teams, auditing the codebase, looking at data.
- **Communication**: a clear written narrative that different audiences understand.
- **Buy-in**: handling sceptics, involving others in shaping it.
- **Execution**: turning vision into milestones, owners and measurable outcomes.
- **Adaptability**: changing course when evidence changes.

## Common variants

- "Where should your front-end architecture be in two years, and how would you get there?"
- "Tell me about a strategy document you wrote that changed how teams worked."
- "How do you align several teams on a long-term technical direction?"
- "What does a good technical vision look like?"
