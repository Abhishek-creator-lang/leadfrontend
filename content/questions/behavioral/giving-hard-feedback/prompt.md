> "Tell me about the most difficult piece of feedback you've had to give someone. How did you approach it, and what happened?"

Leads give feedback constantly, and the hard cases are rarely about code style. They are about behaviour: a talented engineer who is dismissive in reviews, someone who keeps missing commitments, or a peer whose decisions hurt the team. Interviewers want to see that you give feedback promptly, specifically and with care.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Willingness**: do you address problems or avoid them?
- **Timeliness**: did you act soon, or let it build up for months?
- **Specificity**: concrete behaviour and impact, not personality judgements.
- **Delivery**: private, prepared, two-way, open to the other side of the story.
- **Outcome**: did the behaviour change, and how did you follow up?

## Common variants

- "Tell me about a time you had to tell a high performer they were hurting the team."
- "How do you give feedback to someone more senior than you?"
- "Describe a time you gave feedback that wasn't well received."
- "What framework do you use for feedback?"
