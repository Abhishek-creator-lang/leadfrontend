> "Tell me about a time you strongly disagreed with a product manager. What was it about, and how was it resolved?"

Front-end leads sit closest to product and design, so friction with PMs over scope, quality, deadlines and implementation is constant. The interviewer wants to see that you can push back with evidence, understand the PM's goals, and reach a decision without damaging the relationship.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Empathy for the other side**: do you understand the PM's incentives and constraints?
- **Evidence over opinion**: did you bring data, prototypes or user impact rather than "engineering says no"?
- **Options, not ultimatums**: did you offer alternatives and trade-offs?
- **Disagree and commit**: if the decision went against you, did you support it fully?
- **Relationship outcome**: is the partnership stronger afterward?

## Common variants

- "Tell me about a conflict with a non-engineering stakeholder."
- "Describe a time product wanted something you thought was a bad idea."
- "How do you handle a PM who keeps changing requirements mid-sprint?"
- "Tell me about a time you pushed back on a deadline."
