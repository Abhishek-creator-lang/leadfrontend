> "Tell me about a time a stakeholder asked your team for something and you said no, or not yet. How did you handle it, and what happened?"

Leads are expected to protect their team's focus and the product's quality without becoming a blocker. This question tests whether you can disagree upward or sideways and still be seen as a partner.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Judgement**: did you say no for a good reason (risk, cost, user impact), or out of habit or preference?
- **Use of evidence**: data, estimates and trade-offs rather than opinion.
- **Alternatives**: did you offer a "yes, if" or a smaller version that met the real need?
- **Communication**: tone, timing and choosing the right forum.
- **Relationship**: did trust with the stakeholder survive or even grow?
- **Escalation sense**: knowing when to decide yourself and when to bring in your manager.

## Common variants

- "Describe a time you pushed back on a request from leadership."
- "How do you handle a product manager who keeps adding scope?"
- "Tell me about a time you had to protect your team's roadmap."
- "Have you ever refused to ship something? Why?"
