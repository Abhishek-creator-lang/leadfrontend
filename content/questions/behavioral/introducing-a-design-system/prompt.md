> "Have you built or introduced a design system? Tell me how it started, how you got teams to use it, and what difference it made."

Design systems are a classic lead-level initiative because they are as much about people and process as about components. Interviewers want to know whether you treated it as a product with users, or as a component library that nobody adopted.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Product thinking**: who the consumers are, what problem it solves for them, and how you measured value.
- **Design partnership**: working with designers on tokens, Figma libraries and shared ownership.
- **Technical choices**: tokens, theming, accessibility, API design, versioning, distribution.
- **Adoption strategy**: incentives, migration help and governance, not mandates.
- **Sustainability**: contribution model, funding and avoiding a bottleneck team.

## Common variants

- "How would you start a design system at a company that doesn't have one?"
- "Your design system has low adoption. What do you do?"
- "How do you balance consistency with teams' need to move fast?"
- "Tell me about a component library you maintained used by other teams."
