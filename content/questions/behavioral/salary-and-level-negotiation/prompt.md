> "What are your salary expectations?"
>
> Later: "The panel was very positive, but we would like to offer you the senior level rather than lead. How do you feel about that?"

These conversations usually happen with a recruiter rather than in a panel, but they decide much of the outcome. Leveling matters as much as salary: it sets your scope, your next promotion and your pay band for years.

## What the interviewer or recruiter is assessing

- **Professionalism under pressure**: calm, factual, not emotional or adversarial.
- **Market awareness**: do you know the range for the level and location?
- **Clarity about what you want**: level, scope and total compensation, and which matters most.
- **Evidence for your level**: can you point to scope that matches the lead definition?
- **Fit risk**: will you accept and stay, or keep negotiating and leave early?

## Common variants

- "What are you earning now?"
- "What is the minimum you would accept?"
- "We have a fixed budget for this role. Would that work?"
- "Do you have other offers? What would it take for you to sign today?"
- "We think you are strong, but we see you at the level below. Would you consider it?"
