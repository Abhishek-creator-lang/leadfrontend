> "Tell me about a time you received critical feedback. How did you react, and what did you do with it?"

Leads set the tone for how a team handles feedback. If you get defensive, your team learns that feedback is dangerous. Interviewers want a real piece of criticism, an honest account of your first reaction, and evidence that you changed.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Self-awareness**: can you name a real weakness that mattered?
- **Openness**: how you reacted in the moment, and whether you sought to understand.
- **Action**: concrete changes, not just "I took it on board".
- **Verification**: how you checked that the change worked.
- **Modelling**: whether you now invite feedback actively.

## Common variants

- "What's the most useful feedback you've ever received?"
- "Tell me about a time you were surprised by feedback."
- "What would your last manager say you need to work on?"
- "Describe feedback you disagreed with at first."
