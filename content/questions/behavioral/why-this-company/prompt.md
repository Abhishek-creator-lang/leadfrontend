> "Why are you interested in this role, and why us in particular?"

This sounds like small talk, but it is often scored. A generic answer ("great culture, interesting product") tells the interviewer you have not done your homework, and at lead level it raises doubts about how you will approach understanding a business.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Research**: do you understand the product, users and the front-end challenges this company likely has?
- **Fit**: does your experience map onto those challenges?
- **Motivation**: are you moving *towards* something, or just away from your current job?
- **Level awareness**: do you understand what this specific lead role is for?
- **Retention risk**: will you still be engaged in two years?

## Common variants

- "What do you know about us?"
- "Why this role and not a similar one elsewhere?"
- "What attracted you to the job description?"
- "Where do you see yourself contributing in the first year?"
