> "Tell me about a time you handed off something important that you could have done faster yourself. How did you decide, and how did it go?"

Many strong individual contributors struggle with the move to lead because they keep the interesting or risky work for themselves. This question checks whether you multiply the team or bottleneck it.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Judgement about what to delegate**: do you give away real, visible work or only chores?
- **Setting context**: goals, constraints and what "done" means, rather than a list of steps.
- **Support without micromanaging**: check-ins, safety nets and where you step in.
- **Tolerance for a different approach**: can you accept a solution that is not the one you would have chosen?
- **Credit**: who presented the result, and who got recognised.

## Common variants

- "How do you decide what to do yourself and what to give to the team?"
- "Tell me about a time delegation went wrong."
- "What is something you used to do that you no longer do as a lead?"
- "How do you make sure delegated work still meets your quality bar?"
