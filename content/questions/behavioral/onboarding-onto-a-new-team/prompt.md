> "Tell me about a time you joined or took over an existing team as its lead. What did you do in the first 90 days?"

The interviewer is imagining you in your first months at their company. They want to see that you learn before changing things, build trust with the team, and still produce visible impact early.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Listening first**: understanding the team, product, history and why things are the way they are.
- **Relationship building**: with engineers, the manager, product, design and neighbouring teams.
- **Prioritisation**: spotting the few changes that matter most.
- **Early wins**: delivering something useful without disrupting the team.
- **Change management**: introducing changes at a pace the team accepts.

## Common variants

- "What would your first 90 days look like here?"
- "How do you get up to speed on a large unfamiliar codebase?"
- "Tell me about a time you inherited a team with low morale."
- "How do you earn credibility as a new lead?"
