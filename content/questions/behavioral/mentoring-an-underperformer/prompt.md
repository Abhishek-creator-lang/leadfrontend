> "Tell me about a time someone on your team was underperforming. What did you do?"

Leads are expected to notice struggling engineers early, figure out the cause, and either help them succeed or work with their manager on a fair process. The interviewer wants to see care and directness together, not one at the expense of the other.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Early detection**: what signals you noticed and how quickly you acted.
- **Diagnosis before prescription**: skill gap, unclear expectations, wrong role, or something personal?
- **Clear expectations**: specific, measurable goals and timelines.
- **Support**: pairing, scoped work, regular check-ins.
- **Honesty and fairness**: documentation, involving the manager, and handling the outcome either way.
- **Respect**: how you talk about the person.

## Common variants

- "How do you handle a low performer on your team?"
- "Tell me about someone you helped turn around."
- "Describe a time you had to work with an engineer who wasn't meeting the bar."
- "What do you do when a team member's work keeps needing rework?"
