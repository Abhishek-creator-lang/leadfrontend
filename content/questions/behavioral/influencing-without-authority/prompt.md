> "Tell me about a time you needed people who didn't report to you to change how they worked. How did you get them on board?"

At lead and staff level, most of your impact goes through teams you do not manage. This question tests whether you can drive change across an organisation with evidence, empathy and good mechanisms, rather than with a title or by escalation.

## What the interviewer is assessing

- **Understanding of others' incentives**: why the other teams were not already doing it.
- **Making the change easy**: tooling, codemods, documentation, doing the first migration yourself.
- **Evidence and storytelling**: data that matters to the people you need, not only to you.
- **Coalition building**: early adopters, champions and sponsors.
- **Patience and persistence**: driving adoption over months, measuring it, and dealing with holdouts.

## Common variants

- "Describe a cross-team initiative you drove without formal authority."
- "How have you gotten another team to prioritise something important to you?"
- "Tell me about a standard you introduced across multiple teams."
- "How do you lead when you're not the manager?"
