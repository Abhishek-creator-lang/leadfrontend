Design the **browser client for a video conferencing product** like Zoom or Google Meet.

Users join a meeting from a link, pick camera and microphone in a pre-join screen, then see participants in a grid
or speaker layout. They can mute, turn video off, share their screen, chat, raise a hand and see who is speaking.

The interviewer adds:

- Meetings range from 1:1 calls to **100 active participants**, plus webinars with thousands of view-only attendees.
- Users join from laptops on home Wi-Fi, from corporate networks behind strict firewalls, and from phones on mobile
  data.
- Audio quality is the top priority; video should degrade gracefully.
- The app runs in the browser with no install.

Focus on the media architecture (topology and signalling), client components, adaptive quality, layouts, device
handling, and the reliability, accessibility and observability of the client.
