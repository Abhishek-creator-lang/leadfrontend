Design a reusable **image carousel component** for a design system. It is used for product image galleries on
e-commerce pages, listing photos on a rentals site, and promotional hero banners on a home page.

Requirements given by the interviewer:

- Previous/next buttons, slide indicators (dots or thumbnails), and swipe on touch devices.
- Optional autoplay for hero banners; galleries never autoplay.
- Optional infinite looping.
- Show one slide at a time, or several (e.g. 4 product cards per view on desktop, 1.2 on mobile with a peek).
- Images must load fast without wasting bandwidth; the first slide is often the page's largest element.
- Fully accessible.

Discuss the component API, rendering approach, image loading strategy, interactions and accessibility.
